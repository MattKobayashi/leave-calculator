A little context for the numbers

Your balance = leave accrued − leave deducted. Accrual is your annual entitlement × inclusive calendar days in the calculation period ÷ 365. This keeps the original calculator’s fixed 365-day convention, including in leap years.

Your non-working weekdays, selected full-day holidays and custom dates reduce leave deducted, not accrual. Only leave inside the calculation period is deducted. Overlaps belong to the first saved entry. Hours use a 7.6-hour working day.

This is a planning estimate, not a payroll record. It doesn’t include opening balances, unpaid leave, changing entitlements or part-day leave. Check your employer’s records for your official balance.

Your plan is saved in this browser, not stored on the server. Export a copy to keep it safe.
